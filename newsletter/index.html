What You’ll Get

This is a weekly AI newsletter built for business leaders, tech professionals, and AI-curious readers who want the signal without the noise. Each edition is concise, practical, and sent once a week.

A typical issue covers the day’s most significant AI development, a plain-English explanation of why it matters (or doesn’t), and a clear verdict on whether it’s worth your attention. Topics include generative AI tools, large language model releases, AI policy and regulation, machine learning research translated into plain English, and practical AI productivity tips for professionals.

It’s designed to answer one question: what do I actually need to know about AI this week? Not everything — just the things that will change how you work, think, or make decisions.

No spam. No affiliate pressure. Unsubscribe whenever you like — it’s one click.

What a typical edition looks like

📬 AI Edge — Friday

Weekly briefing · Plain English · No hype

Today's signal: Google DeepMind published a new paper on AI-assisted drug discovery that cuts early-stage screening time by roughly 40%. Three things you should know about it — and one reason the headline number is probably too optimistic.

Worth your attention? Yes, if you work in pharma, biotech, or healthcare strategy. Interesting-but-not-urgent for everyone else.

One thing to watch this week: The EU AI Act's high-risk system registration deadline is approaching. If your organisation deploys AI in hiring, credit, or critical infrastructure, this is not optional reading.

That's the flavour. Delivered free, once a week.
