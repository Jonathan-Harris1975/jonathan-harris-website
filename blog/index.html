What this section is for

This is not a content farm and it is not trying to win a prize for the most breathless AI takes on the internet. The point of the blog is to give readers a quick written route into the subjects that matter: what changed, what actually matters, what is overhyped, and where to go next if you want the longer version.

For deeper reading, the catalogue and topic hubs are the next stop. For a weekly scan, the published posts, newsletter, and podcast now work together as the strongest current path.
