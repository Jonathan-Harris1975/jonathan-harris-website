Artificial Intelligence eBooks

Core concepts, breakthroughs, and the bigger picture — for readers who want to understand how modern AI works and where it's heading.

These books cover the foundations and frontiers of artificial intelligence: the history of key breakthroughs, how large language models and machine learning systems actually function, what the shift toward AI-augmented work means for individuals and organisations, and the hard questions about consciousness, safety, and societal impact that researchers are grappling with now.

Written for curious readers who want genuine understanding rather than hype — clear on the science, honest about uncertainty, and always grounded in what the evidence actually supports.

The Dumbening: How AI is Reshaping Our Minds

Examines ai's impact on cognition, exploring how automation and digital reliance may alter human thinking, creativity, and decision-making. Pages: 277.

Climate Intelligence: Harnessing AI for a Greener Future

Artificial intelligence combats climate change with emissions tracking, renewable energy optimization, and predictive environmental modeling for sustainability. Pages: 338.

The Architects of AI_ Pioneers_ Breakthroughs_ and the Road Ahead

Chronicles ai's pioneers and breakthroughs, exploring the technology's history and future potential in shaping society. Pages: 315.
