Education

What AI means for teachers, students, and education systems — personalised learning, accessibility, and the honest trade-offs.

Education eBooks

Practical AI for teaching and learning — where it helps, where it harms, and what 'good' looks like in real classrooms.

AI in education spans adaptive learning platforms and automated grading through to AI-generated content and virtual tutoring. The potential is real, but so are the risks: equity gaps, over-reliance, and assessment integrity are live concerns in schools and universities worldwide. These books examine the evidence — what the research actually shows about AI-assisted learning outcomes, and what thoughtful implementation looks like in practice.

For teachers, school leaders, EdTech professionals, and policymakers who want substance over sales pitch.

AI in Education: Reimagining Learning for Every Student

Artificial intelligence personalizes education with adaptive learning, automated grading, and virtual tutors, making education accessible and tailored for all. Pages: 323.

Game AI Unleashed: From Finite State Machines to Machine Learning

Chronicles ai's evolution in gaming, from simple state machines to advanced machine learning, enhancing gameplay and immersion. Pages: 344.
