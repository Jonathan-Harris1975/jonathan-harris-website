Ethics eBooks

Bias, safety, and responsibility — the trade-offs you can't dodge once AI meets power, money, and human lives.

AI ethics isn't an abstract philosophy exercise — it shows up in hiring algorithms that screen out qualified candidates, facial recognition systems with documented accuracy gaps across demographic groups, and autonomous systems that have to make decisions under uncertainty with no obvious right answer. These books treat ethics as a practical discipline: identifying the actual harms, understanding who bears the costs, and asking what responsible deployment looks like in specific contexts.

For technologists, policymakers, procurement officers, and anyone whose decisions shape how AI systems are built and deployed.
