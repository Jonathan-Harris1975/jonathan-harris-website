Law eBooks

Regulation, liability, evidence, and case studies — how legal systems are reacting to AI, and where the cracks are showing.

AI is forcing fundamental questions in law: Who is liable when an algorithm causes harm? Can AI-generated evidence be admitted in court? How should regulators govern systems that no single person fully understands? These books examine real case studies and emerging legal frameworks, from the EU AI Act to liability debates in common law jurisdictions.

Written for legal professionals, policy researchers, and informed general readers — clear on the law as it stands, candid about what remains unresolved, and focused on practical implications rather than speculation.
