Transportation

Autonomous vehicles, smart logistics, aviation, railways, and maritime — AI's role in moving people and goods more efficiently and safely.

Transportation eBooks

Aviation, shipping, logistics, rail, and automotive — AI in motion, with all the operational and safety baggage that comes with it.

Transport is one of the most demanding environments for AI deployment: safety margins are tight, infrastructure is expensive, and failures are public. These books trace how AI is being applied across every mode — from autonomous vessels and predictive aircraft maintenance to smart rail scheduling and self-driving car development — with honest analysis of where the technology delivers and where it still falls short.

Practical, grounded, and written for readers who need to understand operational realities rather than marketing claims.

AI and Formula 1: Redefining Speed and Strategy with Intelligent Technology

Artificial intelligence enhances formula 1 with real-time strategy optimization, predictive analytics, and improved safety, changing racing performance and team efficiency. Pages: 227.

Artificial Intelligence in Logistics: Optimizing Efficiency and Sustainability

Artificial intelligence streamlines logistics with route optimization, demand forecasting, and automated warehousing, reducing costs and environmental impact for sustainable operations. Pages: 339.

AI in Maritime: Revolutionizing Shipping for Sustainability

Artificial intelligence revolutionizes maritime shipping with autonomous vessels, route optimization, and emissions tracking, fostering sustainable global trade. Pages: 312.

The Autonomous Revolution: Artificial Intelligence and the Future of the Automotive Industry

Artificial intelligence drives the automotive industry with self-driving cars, predictive maintenance, and smart manufacturing, reshaping mobility and safety. Pages: 324.

Artificial Intelligence Revolution in Manufacturing: Modernizing Operations, Maintenance, and Service Delivery

Artificial intelligence modernizes manufacturing with predictive maintenance, automated production, and optimized supply chains, boosting efficiency and innovation. Pages: 369.

AI Revolution in Railways: Modernizing Travel for a Smarter Future

Artificial intelligence modernizes railways with predictive maintenance, autonomous trains, and optimized scheduling, enhancing safety and efficiency. Pages: 254.
