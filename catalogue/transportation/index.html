Transportation eBooks

Aviation, shipping, logistics, rail, and automotive — AI in motion, with all the operational and safety baggage that comes with it.

Transport is one of the most demanding environments for AI deployment: safety margins are tight, infrastructure is expensive, and failures are public. These books trace how AI is being applied across every mode — from autonomous vessels and predictive aircraft maintenance to smart rail scheduling and self-driving car development — with honest analysis of where the technology delivers and where it still falls short.

Practical, grounded, and written for readers who need to understand operational realities rather than marketing claims.
