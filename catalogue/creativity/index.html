Creativity eBooks

How AI is changing music, filmmaking, journalism, games, and the messy human business of making things people actually care about.

The creative industries are undergoing genuine disruption — not the speculative kind, but the kind where working professionals are already changing how they write, compose, animate, and produce. These books examine AI's role across creative sectors with real specificity: how generative tools are being adopted, where they genuinely augment human creativity, and where the ethical and legal tensions around authorship and originality remain unresolved.

For creators, producers, commissioners, and anyone trying to think clearly about what AI means for the future of culture and creative work.
