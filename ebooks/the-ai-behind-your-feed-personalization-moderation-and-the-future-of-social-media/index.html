What makes this book distinct

You probably know that your social media feed is algorithmically curated — but this book explains exactly how, and what the documented consequences are. It covers recommendation systems, content moderation AI, advertising targeting, and the political economy that shapes all three.

Not your book? This book takes a critical perspective. If you're looking for a guide to growing a social media following using AI tools, this isn't it. If you want to understand the systems that shape what billions of people see and believe, it is.
