# Artificial Intelligence for Cyber Security: A Practical Guide to Data Breach Prevention

## Summary

A guide to using artificial intelligence for cybersecurity, using machine learning to detect threats, prevent breaches, and enhance data protection. The focus stays on how AI changes the day-to-day reality of Cyber Security: the tooling, the judgement calls, and the parts that still need a human spine.

## What this book covers

It shows where AI fits inside Cyber Security, what has to change underneath for it to work, where the risks hide, and which outcomes are realistic rather than merely well-marketed.

## Who this book is for

Suits readers who want to understand how AI changes Cyber Security in practice, especially security teams, risk managers, founders, and readers tracking digital defence looking for grounded examples and fewer slogans.

## What you’ll learn

- Where AI is already being used in cyber security today — and where attacks outpace slide decks.
- The working detail is in attack paths, defence, and risk.
- Key themes including threat detection, breach prevention, automation, response.
- The limits, risks, and awkward questions worth asking before you sign off on the sales pitch.

## Why it matters

AI is not arriving in Cyber Security as a parlour trick. It changes how organisations handle breach risk, response speed, and resilience, so the boring details matter more than the slogans.
