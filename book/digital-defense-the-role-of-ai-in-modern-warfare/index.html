Why it matters

AI in defence is one of the areas where hype is most dangerous. The decisions involve surveillance, autonomy, escalation risk, cybersecurity, logistics, and real-world human consequences. A clear view beats futuristic chest-beating every time.

What you’ll learn

  • How AI is used in military intelligence, autonomous systems, logistics, and cyber defence.
  • Where the practical benefits are — and where the risks and limitations start to bite.
  • Why ethics, accountability, and human oversight matter in defence applications.
  • How to think sensibly about modern warfare without getting lost in science-fiction fluff.

Who this book is for

Readers interested in defence, cybersecurity, national security, public policy, and applied AI who want a plain-English explanation without technical waffle.

What this book covers

Artificial intelligence transforms warfare with autonomous drones, predictive intelligence, and cybersecurity, reshaping military strategy and ethics.

This is the part of the AI conversation that often gets reduced to either Hollywood paranoia or chest-thumping sales copy. The useful middle ground is understanding where AI genuinely changes reconnaissance, targeting support, cyber operations, logistics, and decision support — while keeping human accountability firmly on the table.

Not your book? Not a military hardware manual and not a work of breathless techno-propaganda. This is a practical AI book about what the systems do, how they are used, and why the trade-offs matter.
