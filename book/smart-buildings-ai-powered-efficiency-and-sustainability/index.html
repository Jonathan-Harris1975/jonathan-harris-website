What makes this book distinct

AI is already invisible in most films you've watched — doing de-ageing, digital stunt work, crowd simulation, and colour grading at scale. This book shows how extensively AI is embedded in modern production pipelines, and examines the much bigger questions that generative AI raises for the creative and labour structures of the film industry.

Not your book? Written for property professionals, sustainability directors, and facilities decision-makers — not IoT engineers wanting technical specifications. The focus is on what AI actually changes about how buildings are managed and what the economics look like.
