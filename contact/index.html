Get in Touch

If you’ve got something worth discussing, this is the place. Clear details beat noise every time.

What is this page for?

Use this page for sensible enquiries about the newsletter, podcast, ebooks, speaking, partnerships, or practical AI questions tied to the work on the site. Clear context helps. Vague "let's connect" notes, like most of civilisation's networking rituals, tend to achieve less.

If your message is relevant, expect a reply path through the form or the hosted fallback link below. The direct form is there for browsers that decide embeds are morally offensive.

Quick answers

What should I include in my message?

The reason for your enquiry, the page, book, or project you are referring to, and the outcome you want. That saves everyone a round of interpretive email theatre.

What kinds of enquiry make sense here?

Questions about the site, ebook catalogue, podcast, newsletter, interviews, collaborations, and practical AI discussion tied to Jonathan Harris's work.

What if the embedded form does not work?

Use the hosted JotForm link. It goes to the same contact flow without relying on the embedded version.
