Privacy Policy

Plain English: what I collect, why I collect it, and what I do (and don’t do) with your data.

Last updated: January 2026

Policy sections

1. Who I Am

This site is operated by me, Jonathan Harris — an independent author exploring artificial intelligence and emerging technologies.

2. What Information I Collect

Data is only collected when you choose to submit it — for example, newsletter sign‑ups or contact forms. That typically means your name and email address.

3. How Your Data Is Used

Your data is used to deliver what you asked for. I don’t sell, rent, or hand it to third parties for marketing.

4. Cookies

This site uses essential cookies for core functionality and anonymous analytics cookies via CookieYes and Metricool. You can manage cookies via the banner (where shown) or your browser settings.

5. Third‑Party Services

This website uses a small set of third‑party services to keep things running (for example: analytics, cookie consent, and embedded forms). Each provider operates under their own privacy policy.

6. Data Retention

I keep personal data only as long as it’s needed to provide the service you requested (for example: ongoing newsletter delivery). You can unsubscribe at any time.

7. Your Rights

You can request access, correction, or deletion of your data. Email hello@jonathan-harris.online.

8. Updates to This Policy

If this policy changes, I’ll update this page and the “Last updated” date above.
